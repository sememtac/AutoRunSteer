# AutoRunSteer

## v1.2.0

First public release.

- Tap and hold the autorun button (default 0.5 s) to steer the autorun with the camera stick; a quick
  tap is a plain autorun.
- Short controller rumble and an "Autorun steering on" message when steering turns on.
- Steering ends with the autorun: stopping, toggling autorun off, or pressing forward/back.
- Works whichever button autorun is bound to, including when the game handles the autorun itself.
- `/autorunsteer on|off|hold <seconds>|status|debug`.
